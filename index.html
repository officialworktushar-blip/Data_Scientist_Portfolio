How I Work

From Question to Shipped System

Five steps I run on every engagement — no black boxes, no science projects.

  1. 01

    Frame the problem

    Agree the decision, the metric that moves it, and what "done" looks like.

  2. 02

    Data audit

    Profile sources, leakage risks and gaps before a single model is trained.

  3. 03

    Model and experiment

    Strong baselines first, then complexity only where it earns its keep.

  4. 04

    Ship to production

    Versioned pipelines, CI, monitoring and rollback — not a notebook in a zip.

  5. 05

    Monitor and iterate

    Drift alerts, retraining triggers and a written handover your team owns.
