Healthcare AI — Kaiser Permanente

Patient Risk Prediction Engine: End-to-End ML for Healthcare

Kaiser Permanente needed to reduce costly 30-day hospital readmissions. Working embedded with their clinical data team, I designed and deployed a gradient boosting ensemble that analyzes 340+ patient features — vitals, medication history, social determinants, and EHR data — to identify high-risk patients at discharge.

The model achieved a 94% AUC and was integrated directly into clinical workflows via a HIPAA-compliant API, enabling care coordinators to intervene proactively. In Year 1, readmission rates dropped 62% in target populations, generating $8M in savings.
